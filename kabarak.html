KABARAK UNIVERSITY

ABOUT

Kabarak University is a private Christian-based institution located in Kenya. It is committed to academic excellence, integrity, and service to society.

History

The university was established to provide holistic education grounded in Christian values.

Location

Kabarak University is located in Nakuru County along the Nakuru–Eldama Ravine Highway.

ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

Academic programs at Kabarak University are offered through the following schools:

  1. School of Education and Social Sciences
  2. School of Science, Engineering and Technology
  3. School of Health Sciences
  4. School of Business and Economics
  5. School of Pharmacy
  6. School of Law
  7. School of Music and Media
